01 · Pricing intelligence

Pricing Intelligence Model

Checking our prices against the market meant collecting competitor prices by hand — hours of work, often out of date by the time it was done.

  • Scrapes prices from top competitors automatically
  • Suggests prices using more than one method and shows where we stand vs. the market
  • Flags SKUs priced above market, and SKUs bought above competitors' selling price to trigger supplier negotiation
ImpactHours → under 10 minutes
PythonWeb ScrapingHTML / JS
02 · Purchasing decision support

Purchasing Intelligence Model

Buying decisions depended on many inputs at once — cost, selling price, margin target, deal terms, and market prices — and were easy to get wrong.

  • Tells the buyer whether to buy at all: Buy / Conditional / Hold / Review
  • Recommends the best supplier and buy price
  • Compares against the nearest competitor and market average, then suggests the selling price
ImpactA safeguard against wrong purchases
Decision LogicHTML / JSAI-assisted Dev
03 · Supply & demand planning

Replenishment Planner

Every morning the daily stock sheet had to be read line by line to decide what to order, from whom, and for which warehouse.

  • Upload the daily stock sheet and see every SKU flagged: order today, running low, safe, or dead stock
  • Suggests order quantity per warehouse for a chosen number of days of cover
  • Ranks vendors to call, flags OOS-with-demand and warehouse imbalances, and exports a ready PO per vendor
ImpactDaily stock sheet → vendor-ready orders in minutes
HTML / JSPlanningArabic / English
04 · Supply & demand planning

Dynamic Safety Stock & Promo Planning

Promo SKUs sold out before campaigns ended, or stock was locked away that could have been sold.

  • Uses current stock, run-rate history, warehouse, and campaign end date
  • Run-rate fallback: 7 → 15 → 30 → 90 days when data is thin
  • Refreshes daily and outputs upload-ready files
ImpactSelected SKUs stay available through campaigns
PlanningRun RateHTML / JS
05 · Master data automation

Odoo SKU Data Enrichment & Catalog Suite

Creating SKUs in Odoo was manual — about 30 minutes for every 10 products — and easy to get wrong.

  • Turns product barcodes into import-ready Odoo sheets
  • Maps brand, category, and ETA automatically, and reports issues before upload
  • Applies bulk status, classification, and visibility changes
Impact30 min / 10 SKUs → large batches in minutes
Odoo ERPMaster DataHTML / JS
06 · Internal commerce

Employee Offers Store

Staff offers were shared informally, with no clear catalog, no consistent prices, and no record of who ordered what.

  • A mobile-friendly store where MNT-Halan employees browse products by category at 50% off
  • Employees build their selection and confirm with name and phone — no minimum, pickup and cash at the warehouse
  • Orders reach the warehouse team in one place, with printable order summaries
ImpactEmployee offers run through one simple store
